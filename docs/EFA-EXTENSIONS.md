## Method decisions under qualification

- Native TypeScript/browser workers remain the application runtime; R remains development/CI only.
- Ordinal MICE follows the pinned R `mice.impute.polr` convention. Binary items follow `mice.impute.logreg`. Their stochastic steps differ and must be tested with saved random draws rather than assumed cross-language seed identity.
- References: https://amices.org/mice/reference/mice.impute.polr.html and https://lavaan.ugent.be/tutorial/cat.html.
- Pattern and structure have explicit controls and default heatmaps. The assistant-guided item workflow remains a later stage after numerical qualification.

## Reference limitations retained explicitly

- The MICE reference image sources the unmodified pinned conditional functions in a fixed FCS driver; it does not claim identical execution of the entire installed `mice` sampler or a `mids` object.
- For the extreme separated ordinal fixture, raw MASS probability subtraction loses precision in cells around `1e-13`. Its original outputs remain in the fixture; a separately recorded R numerical-derivative optimization of the same log-space likelihood supplies the stable reference. Ordinary fixtures retain direct MASS parity and saved categorical draws match the original conditional method.
- Bootstrap intervals are descriptive percentile intervals after sign/permutation matching. Weak factor separation or substantial failed fits can undermine interpretation; failed fits remain visible and make summaries provisional.
